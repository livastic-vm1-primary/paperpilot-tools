Engineer checklist

Confirm the endpoint returns a 402 containing the correct price, network, asset and payee. Compare the free mock structure against your expected 20-field diff. After an explicitly authorized purchase, check the two block numbers, the 20 source-specific before/after values, public explorer contracts and evidence timestamps. Snapshot differences cannot prove an event happened only once between those blocks. Historical states can differ because of real contract changes, proxy upgrades or assumptions in the API's field list. Treat any missing or failed upstream read as missing data, not as a zero difference.
