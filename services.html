Share the result you need, output formats, rough budget and deadline. Please do not send confidential source files or credentials in the first email.

03Verify the arrangement

Only proceed when the customer and owner have accepted the scope and a legitimate payment/identity route is available. No crypto deposits to an unbacked-up wallet.

Working source, usage documentation, tests and known limitations as specified in the agreement. No claims of success without actual validation.
