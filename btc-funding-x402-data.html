What the response tells you and what it does not

Funding rates are periodic transfers between long and short perpetual positions. Venues may use different funding intervals, funding caps, marks and timestamps. A reported rate is not APY, savings interest, guaranteed return, an investment recommendation, an instruction to trade or proof that a proposed basis trade is profitable. Compare the source interval and mark information, not merely the sign of the rate.

The provider attempts four independent public venues and does not charge for a partial snapshot. Data can still be delayed between the upstream quote, this service, your own analysis and a trade. Verify directly with the exchange before any position. Do not provide trading authority or private wallet credentials to this market-data endpoint.
