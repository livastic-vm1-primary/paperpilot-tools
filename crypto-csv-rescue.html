Paired with explicit link

Sender and receiver have matching asset, amount and supplied transfer reference. This is a candidate, not proof of the same owner or an actual blockchain transfer.

Review the mismatch

A related transfer has different quantities, asset type, transaction reference, missing one side, or too many possible legs.

Need original purchase evidence

An exported disposal lacks an explicit acquisition source reference. We ask for records, not an invented cost basis.

Potential repeated export

Identical rows or reused trade IDs are flagged. Legitimate partial fills can also repeat references; nothing is automatically deleted.
