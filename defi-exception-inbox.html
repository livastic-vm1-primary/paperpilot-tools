What this catches

  • Possible swaps and LP activity: hints from a declared method/action string, not a decoded token swap.
  • Possible rewards, lending and bridges: flag ambiguity for human inspection.
  • Multiple events sharing one transaction hash: note potential double-counting instead of silently removing rows.
  • Missing or malformed source fields: hash, date, amount, header mismatch.
  • Same sending/receiving address: a review clue, not proof of a self-transfer or no tax liability.

What this does not do

  • No live wallet syncing, blockchain verification or DeFi protocol ABI decoding.
  • No token prices, realized gains, cost basis, yields, tax law or loss calculations.
  • No automated labeling of swaps as disposals or bridging as nontaxable.
  • No wallet connect, transaction signing, swaps, investment recommendations or token approval requests.
  • No guarantee that a source CSV is complete or correct.
